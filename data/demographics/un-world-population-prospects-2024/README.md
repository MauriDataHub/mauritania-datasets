# UN World Population Prospects 2024 — Mauritania (estimates and medium-variant projections)

## Processing

Filtered three global CSV files (Demographic Indicators; Population by 5-year age group and sex, 1 July; Population on 1 January by 5-year age group and sex) to `ISO3_code` = MRT. Columns unchanged.

## Limitations

Rows with `Variant` = Medium beyond 2023 are projections. Population counts are in thousands (see WPP documentation).
