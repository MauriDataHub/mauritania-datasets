# WorldPop — Gridded population and density, Mauritania 2020 (UN-adjusted)

Gridded estimates of population for 2020 adjusted to UN WPP national totals: population count at 1 km (aggregated from 100 m, unconstrained), population density at 1 km, and population count at ~100 m constrained to built-up settlement areas.

## Processing

GeoTIFF files downloaded unchanged from data.worldpop.org.

## Coordinate reference system

Geographic WGS 84 (EPSG:4326), embedded in each GeoTIFF.

## Limitations

Modelled estimates (random-forest dasymetric mapping); uncertainty is highest in sparsely populated desert areas.
