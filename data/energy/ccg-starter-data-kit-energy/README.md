## Limitations

## Notes

Starter Data Kit tables for energy-system modelling of Mauritania (OSeMOSYS/MAED inputs): demand, supply, technologies, costs, renewable potentials. The large SAND model workbooks (*.xlsm, 43 MB each) are not mirrored — see the Zenodo record.
