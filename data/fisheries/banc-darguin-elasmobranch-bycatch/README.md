# Replication Data for: Overfishing of threatened bycatch species in a marine protected area: The elasmobranchs of Banc d’Arguin, Mauritania

These data contain the catch, effort, and gear data for Elasmobranch catches in the Parc National du Banc d’Arguin, Mauritania, on which the figures were based for the publication by Lemrabott et al. in Fisheries Management and Ecology.
