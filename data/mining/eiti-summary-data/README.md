# EITI — Extractive-industry summary data for Mauritania (2005–2022)

Annual EITI summary-data workbooks for Mauritania reporting government revenues from the extractive sector (mining and oil/gas): revenue streams by government agency and by company, production and export figures and contextual disclosures, plus a CSV of the headline figures of each annual record.

## Processing

Workbooks downloaded unchanged from each `report_file` link of the EITI API summary-data records MR2005–MR2022. `eiti_summary_records.csv` flattens the scalar fields of those API records (period, currency, exchange rate, government and company revenue totals, sector flags).

## Limitations

Figures are reconciled reports prepared by the national EITI committee; units are in the reporting currency (see `currency`, `currency_rate`). Workbook template versions change over time.
