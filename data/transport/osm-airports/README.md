# Airports of Mauritania

Airports and aviation infrastructure: airports, heliports, helipads, runways, terminals, and emergency landing sites tagged via aeroway, building, or emergency. Useful for logistics, evacuation routing, and aerial-response planning. Coverage reflects where volunteer mappers have been active. Urban areas are usually well represented, remote areas may be sparse. Cross-check critical decisions with local field knowledge. - Source: OpenStreetMap, a free, collaborative world map - Tags: aeroway, building, emergency - Part of the country exports on HDX by HOT
